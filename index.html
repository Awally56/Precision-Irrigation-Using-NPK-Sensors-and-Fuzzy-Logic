AGRONOMIC FORMULATION

Core FAO-56 Mathematical Reference Equations

Hydrological and bio-physical formulas implemented in irrigation/agro.py (Allen et al., 1998)

Nineveh Latitude: 36.34°N
1. Hargreaves-Samani ET0 Model
ET₀ = 0.0023 · Rₐ · (T_mean + 17.8) · √(T_max - T_min)

Calculates reference evapotranspiration (mm/day) using air temperature only. Extraterrestrial radiation Rₐ is computed from solar declination δ and sunset hour angle ωₛ at latitude 36.34°N. Eliminates need for costly net radiation pyranometers.

FAO-56 Eq. 52 · Hargreaves & Samani (1985)
2. Crop Evapotranspiration (ETc)
ET_c = K_c · ET₀

Calculates daily water demand based on winter wheat phenology. K_c curve: Initial = 0.70 (25d), Development = 0.70→1.15 (100d), Mid-Season = 1.15 (40d, heading/flowering peak), Late = 1.15→0.30 (30d, ripening).

FAO-56 Fig. 25 & Table 12 (Wheat)
3. Root-Zone Water Depletion (Dr)
D_r = 1000 · (θ_fc - θ_vwc) · Z_r
RAW = p · TAW = 0.55 · [1000 · (θ_fc - θ_wp) · Z_r]

Nineveh clay-loam properties: Field Capacity θ_fc = 0.32, Wilting Point θ_wp = 0.17. Stress threshold occurs when depletion D > 55% of TAW. Root depth Z_r expands dynamically from 0.30 m to 1.20 m.

  • • 12V DC Input: Solar panel + 12V 7Ah backup battery.
  • • LM2596 Step-Down: Supplies 5V for Relay & Sensors.
  • • ESP32 LDO: Regulates 3.3V logic rail.
  • • Power Gate (GPIO 25): Switches power to moisture probes only during 300ms sampling window to prevent copper electrolysis.

  • • 7-in-1 Soil NPK: Modbus RTU via MAX485 on UART2 (GPIO 16 RX, 17 TX, 4 DE/RE).
  • • DHT22 Ambient: Air Temp & RH via GPIO 27 (1-wire).
  • • YF-S201 Flow: Hall sensor on GPIO 14 (450 p/L, hardware interrupt).
Median of 15 samples per reading

  • • Hydraulic Output: 28 L/min calibrated flow over 100 m² drip zone.
  • • Local Failsafe: Saturation auto-shutoff (S1+S2 < 1380 ADC), 240m max timer, no-flow alarm.

4-Tier Embedded Safety & Autonomous Failsafe Architecture

Tier 1: Hardware Watchdog & Power Brownout Protection

The solenoid valve is Normally Closed (NC). During brownouts, system reboots, or power failures, the 12V coil automatically de-energizes, shutting off water instantaneously. ESP32 pins initialize to high-impedance input with internal pull-downs, eliminating accidental floating relay triggers.

Tier 2: Shallow Soil Saturation Auto-Cutoff

During any active irrigation cycle, the firmware scans shallow probes (S1, S2 at 15 cm) every 5 seconds. If the raw ADC falls below SAT_STOP_RAW = 1380 (indicating saturation / water table ponding), the valve is forced closed immediately with event tag soil saturated.

Tier 3: Hall-Effect No-Flow Alarm & Pump Cutoff

If the valve is signaled OPEN but fewer than 10 rotor pulses occur within 60 seconds (NO_FLOW_ALARM_S), the firmware immediately trips a failsafe shutoff. This protects against an empty storage tank, a burst pipe before the meter, or a mechanically jammed solenoid valve.

Tier 4: Offline Server Fallback Mode

If communication with the MQTT scheduler is lost for more than 48 hours (FALLBACK_AFTER_H), the ESP32 switches into autonomous edge threshold mode: checking deep root probes (S3, S4 at 40 cm), and applying a conservative emergency pulse (30 min) to prevent permanent crop wilting.
